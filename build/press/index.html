Long bio

RYLAND is a singer, songwriter and producer from Portland, Oregon. He writes and produces his own music, and it moves between alt-pop, indie and electronic without settling. His songs read like journal entries, sometimes literally: on his birthday, September 29, 2024, he released the album *Showing Friends, ten short songs each titled with the date it was made.

2026 has been his busiest year yet, with the singles "everlasting," "be that girl again," "a couple nights ago," "get into my face," "fine by me (2022 demo)," "Summer Nights" and "looking for you." "be that girl again" is his most-played song, and "Denial," from 2023, keeps finding new listeners.

RYLAND is independent and releases his music through DistroKid. Verified on Spotify, he reached about 91,000 monthly listeners in October 2026. His home on the internet is an old iPhone you can open at myphone.digital.
