Long bio

RYLAND writes the songs, builds the tools, and rethinks the way people enter the work. He is an independent artist from Portland, Oregon, and music is where most people meet him first.

The sound. Dark pop he writes and produces: heavy drums and low end underneath, a soft, close voice on top.

The archive. On his birthday, September 29, 2024, he released *Showing Friends: ten short songs, each titled with the date it was made. The track list reads like a diary, and the dates are real.

The device. That interest in form doesn't stop at records. He built his website, myphone.digital, as a working old iPhone: it boots, the apps open, and not everything inside it has been released. He builds his own software tools the same way, as part of one world.

The practice. Music is one medium in a larger practice. In his words: "I'm spiritually growing, and I want to share and expand."

The record so far. No label, releasing through DistroKid. Six releases in 2026, led by "be that girl again," his most-played song. "Denial" (2023) keeps finding listeners, and the newest, "looking for you," arrived September 24, 2026. Verified by Spotify, with about 91,000 monthly listeners as of October 25, 2026.

On Spotify, his bio starts with two words: "guerrilla warfare."
