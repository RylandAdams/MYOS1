Sync licensing outlets: a guide

Who places independent artists' music in TV, film, ads, trailers and games: what each outlet is, what it releases, its deal terms and how to submit. Researched November 2026.

How a placement happens

  1. A show, brand or trailer house sends a brief ("moody indie pop, no lyrics about cars, $5k all in").
  2. Agencies and libraries pitch a few tracks; the music supervisor shortlists and asks for a quote.
  3. The licensee signs two licenses: sync (the song) and master (the recording). One party controlling both is "one-stop" and wins ties.
  4. The production files a cue sheet with the PROs.
  5. The upfront fee (minus the agent's cut) comes soon; PRO backend 6 months to 3 years later.

Deal terms to know

  • Exclusive is the norm for big libraries: the track can't be with any other library or agent.
  • The library becomes the publisher and takes the publisher's half of the PRO money; the writer keeps the writer's half. Any library that takes part of the writer's share is a red flag.
  • Sync and master fees are often split 50/50 between library and writer; some pay upfront and keep all fees.
  • Blanket licenses: many TV clients pay one yearly fee for a whole catalog. That fee usually isn't shared, so the writer's income is the PRO backend from cue sheets.
  • Retitling is how non-exclusive libraries collect: they register the song under a new title.

Artist sync agency

Agencies represent an artist's released songs and pitch them to music supervisors.

Bodega Sync

Indie sync agency · part of Symphonic

What it is
Represents independent artists' released songs for sync: pitches them to music supervisors for TV, film and ads, and lists its catalog on DISCO. One-stop catalog (master and publishing cleared together), which wins ties because it clears in a single call.
Catalog
Artist songs, vocals preferred. Authentic artists over production-library tracks.
Deal
2-year exclusive representation on the songs you list (only those songs), 30% of the sync fees Bodega brings in, no cut of PRO (performance) royalties. You don't need to distribute through Symphonic.
How to submit
Online application at bodegasync.com/apply. At least 10 songs. No samples, marketplace beats or generated music. Review takes about 5 business days.
Apply
bodegasync.com/apply

APM Music and its partner libraries

APM Music (Associated Production Music, Hollywood) is a joint venture of Sony Music Publishing and Universal Music Publishing Group, founded in 1983, with a catalog of over 1,000,000 tracks. It licenses music to TV, film, trailers, games and NFL Films. APM doesn't sign composers itself: it is the licensing agent for partner libraries, and each library picks its own writers. Its composer submission page lists the libraries currently taking music. Rule for all of them: you must own or control 100% of the master and publishing, with clearance from every performer. APM's composer submission page.

Kinetik Muzik

APM's artist roster ("The Artist Collective")

What it is
APM's newest roster, made up "entirely" of independent artists rather than commissioned composers. Recent artists: dark indie rock with emotive pop (Alice Osian), tender R&B (Ash Minor), dreamy indie pop (Love, Sophie), sentimental pop (Lucy Frost). APM's featured placement: Blondfire's "Get You High" in the season 5 trailer of Emily in Paris.
Catalog
Vocal songs by real, touring or recording indie artists. The one door on APM's page built for artists.
Deal
Not published. Its own artists describe it as "an exclusive label representing authentic indie artists": assume exclusive for the songs signed.
How to submit
An artist or band with a following, who releases music commercially and plays live when possible; controls their rights and has a publishing entity with a PRO (ASCAP, BMI or SESAC). Up to 3 tracks as streaming links; stems must be available for every submission; bio plus website, socials and store links.
