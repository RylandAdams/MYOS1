About RYLAND

RYLAND is a singer, songwriter and producer from Portland, Oregon. He writes and produces his own music, and it lives after dark: heavy drums and low end under a soft, close voice, somewhere between alt-pop, R&B and electronic. His song titles work like a diary, and on his album “*Showing Friends” each song is titled with the date it was made.
